## Key interpretation limits

- A vulnerability layer describes susceptibility; it does not show that contamination is present. A hazard layer should locate a source or event. Risk needs hazard, pathway, receptor and evidence, with uncertainty stated.
- Coarse maps and old coordinates can be misleading when matched to individual boreholes. Confirm projection, scale, positional accuracy, date, lineage, class definitions and local validation.
- The app's distance screening is geometry-based. Avoid using arbitrary buffers or point-to-polygon distance as a substitute for hydrogeological flow/pathway analysis.
- The supplied catchment and hydrogeology files are available in GIS as reference layers, and the borehole inventory can be imported as unverified historical records. The borehole inventory provides historical depth, water-level and yield attributes; its supplied chemical fields are empty. External portals are linked for broader groundwater information. No verified contamination or groundwater-risk layer is supplied, so no hazard scoring is inferred from these files.
